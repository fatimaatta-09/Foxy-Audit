Trust Model

Every audit event in this report is anchored to the event before it via a sequential SHA-256 hash chain: Hn = SHA256(datan ‖ Hn-1) . A modification that does not also rewrite every later chain value breaks the chain at the changed row. To make a full rewrite independently detectable, retain a copy of the root hash outside the system or confirm a public-chain anchor shown in this report.
