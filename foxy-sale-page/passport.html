Compliance Passport

Months of AI activity, one shareable proof.

The passport is the artifact you hand an auditor, a customer, or a regulator — a single document that summarizes your verified trail and lets them check it themselves.

Instead of exporting raw logs and hoping they're believed, you export a compliance passport: a structured record of your hash chain, its verdicts, and its integrity status, mapped to the frameworks reviewers already use.

What's inside

  • Mapped to standards: aligned to NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act so reviewers recognize it on sight.
  • Re-verifiable: the recipient confirms the chain is intact on their side — no trust in us required.
  • No raw payloads: it carries hashes, verdicts, and metadata — never your prompts or responses.
  • Point-in-time: export whenever you need — for an audit, a security review, or a customer's due diligence.
