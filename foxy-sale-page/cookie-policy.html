Cookie Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains the cookies and similar local-storage technologies Foxy Audit uses, why, and how you can control them. We use no advertising or third-party tracking cookies — an auditing product has no business embedding trackers.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a site stores in your browser to remember things between requests and visits — for example, that you are securely logged in, or that you dismissed this banner. "Local storage" and "session storage" work similarly but are read only by the site's own code. We refer to all of these together as "cookies" below.

2. Categories we use

Strictly necessary — always on

Required for the site to work securely. These cannot be switched off, because without them you could not log in, stay logged in, or be protected against cross-site request forgery.

Analytics — optional

Help us understand which pages are used and whether anything is slow or broken. Ours are first-party and cookieless: we count page views on our own servers and, only with your permission, store an anonymous visitor id locally. No data is sent to any third party.

Functional — optional

Would remember interface preferences (such as a dashboard layout or time zone). We do not use any functional cookies yet; the category is listed so the choice is available if we add them.

4. What we do not use

  • No advertising or marketing cookies.
  • No third-party tracking pixels (no Meta, Google Ads, or data brokers).
  • No cross-site tracking or fingerprinting.

5. How analytics works

When analytics is enabled, each page view sends a small first-party request to our own backend containing the page path and, if you consented, an anonymous visitor/session id. Your IP address and browser user-agent are one-way hashed (HMAC) on our server and never stored in raw form. There is no third-party analytics provider.

6. Managing your choices

On your first visit you are asked to choose. You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link in our footer, which reopens the consent panel. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though strictly-necessary cookies are required for login to work.

7. Your regional rights

EU / EEA / UK (GDPR): non-essential cookies (analytics) are off until you opt in, and rejecting is as easy as accepting.

California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information. You can opt out of analytics at any time via the preferences panel.

We may update this policy as the product changes; the version and date above will move. Questions? Email privacy@foxyaudit.tech.
