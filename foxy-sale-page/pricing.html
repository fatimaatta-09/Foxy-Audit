By default, the SDK keeps raw prompt and response text on your machine and sends content-blind commitments plus operational metadata. Do not add raw content to optional metadata fields or support requests.

On Pro and Max — and on a free account — you do: you bring your own Gemini or OpenAI key, add it in the dashboard, and those model calls land on your provider bill at your rates. Audit-event credits cover capture, chaining, anchoring and exports; they are not model tokens. Premium can either keep using its own key or switch to Foxy's managed keys, where the model calls are on us. Your key is stored encrypted, used only in memory at grading time, and never shown back to you or written into your audit chain. See the judge.
