Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026 · Version 1.0

Foxy Audit builds tamper-evident audit trails for AI. Trust is the product, so we collect as little as possible and never sell your data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the rights you have.

1. Who we are

Foxy Audit ("we", "us") provides the Foxy Audit platform — a marketing site, a customer dashboard, an SDK, and a desktop companion. For privacy questions, contact privacy@foxyaudit.tech.

2. What we collect

From the marketing site

  • Contact & sign-up details you submit in a form — your email, and any name, company, or message you include.
  • Anonymous usage — page views, with your IP address and browser user-agent one-way hashed (HMAC) on our servers, never stored raw. First-party analytics only, and only the persistent visitor id is set with your consent.
  • Consent records — the choice you make on the cookie banner, with a timestamp and hashed IP/UA, so we can demonstrate consent was obtained.

From the SDK / product

  • Audit metadata, not content. The SDK hashes your AI prompt and response on your machine (SHA-256) and discards the raw text. Only digests, a token count, the policy tag, and an optional model name are sent to us. We never receive your prompts or responses.

Account data

  • Your email and a securely hashed password; billing status if you subscribe.

3. How we use it

  • To provide and secure the service (authentication, the audit chain, verification).
  • To respond to your enquiries and support requests.
  • To understand and improve site performance (first-party analytics, with consent).
  • To process payments and meet legal/accounting obligations.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

We rely on performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for), legitimate interests (securing the service, basic aggregate analytics, responding to enquiries), consent (optional analytics), and legal obligation (tax/accounting).

5. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for the full list. In short: strictly-necessary cookies for login and security, and — only with your permission — first-party, cookieless analytics. No advertising or third-party trackers.

6. Who we share with (sub-processors)

We do not sell your personal information. We share the minimum necessary with vetted providers:

  • Brevo — sends transactional and support email on our behalf.
  • Stripe — processes payments; card details go directly to Stripe, not to us.
  • Our hosting/infrastructure provider — runs the servers and database.

7. Retention

We keep account and audit data for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal, security, or accounting reasons. Anonymous, hashed analytics and consent records are kept on an aggregate basis.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; API keys are peppered and hashed; tenant data is isolated with row-level security; audit records are cryptographically chained so tampering is detectable. IP/UA identifiers are hashed, never stored raw.

9. Your rights

EU / EEA / UK (GDPR): you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and object to certain processing, and you can withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you may request to know, delete, or correct your information, and to opt out of "sale"/"sharing" — though we do not sell or share personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, email privacy@foxyaudit.tech. You can also delete your account and its data from your dashboard.

10. International transfers & children

Your data may be processed in countries other than yours; where required we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Our services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes & contact

We may update this policy as the product evolves; the version and date above will change. Contact us at privacy@foxyaudit.tech.

This document is provided as a good-faith description of our current practices and is not legal advice. Have it reviewed by qualified counsel before relying on it for compliance.
