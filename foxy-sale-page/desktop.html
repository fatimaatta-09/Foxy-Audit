Desktop app

Your compliance officer, on your desktop.

A pixel-art fox that lives at the edge of your screen and reacts the moment an AI call lands — so you feel your compliance state instead of checking a dashboard.

The desktop companion connects to your audit trail and animates as events are captured. It can show local activity immediately, then surface the clean or breach verdict after backend grading — the same verdict sealed into your hash chain.

What it does

  • Live reactions: immediate capture activity, followed by green or red when the backend verdict is available.
  • Chat copilot: ask it about a flagged call or your policy without leaving your desktop.
  • One-click dashboard: jump from the pet straight into your full audit trail.
  • Always ambient: it sits quietly at the bottom of the screen and only speaks up when something matters.
