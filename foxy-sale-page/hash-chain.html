Hash Chain

Tamper-evident by construction.

Your audit trail isn't a log you have to trust — it's a cryptographic chain where any edit to the past breaks everything after it.

Each record stores the SHA-256 of the call it describes plus the hash of the record before it. The hashes link into a single chain, the way blocks link on a blockchain — no external ledger required.

That one design choice is what makes the trail tamper-evident. Change a prompt, a verdict, a timestamp, or delete an entry, and its hash no longer matches — which means the next record's stored "previous hash" no longer matches either, and so on all the way to the tip. The break is obvious and points straight at what was altered.

What that gives you

  • Append-only in practice: the only way to keep the chain valid is to never rewrite history.
  • Model provenance baked in: each hash folds in which model produced the call, so you can't quietly swap what answered.
  • Independently re-computable: anyone with the chain can recompute it and confirm it's intact — no access to us needed.
  • Outage-safe: the chain is append-only, so a backend outage never corrupts what's already recorded.
