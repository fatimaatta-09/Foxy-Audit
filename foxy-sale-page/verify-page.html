Verify Chain

Proof your auditor checks themselves.

The whole point of a hash chain is that no one has to take your word for it. Anyone you share the trail with can re-verify it independently.

Verification recomputes every hash in the chain from the records themselves and checks each link. If the recomputed tip matches, the trail is intact. If a single record was changed, verification fails and shows exactly where.

Because the math is public and the chain is self-contained, your auditor doesn't need an account, special software, or any trust in Foxy Audit — they run the same check we do and get the same answer.

Why it holds up in a review

  • One click, live: re-verification runs on demand, not on a schedule you control.
  • No trust required: the check depends on the records, not on our say-so.
  • Pinpoints tampering: a failed verify names the first record that doesn't match.
  • Shareable: hand over a compliance passport and they verify it on their side.
