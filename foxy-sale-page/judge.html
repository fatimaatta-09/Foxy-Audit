Active-Defense Judge

A second model watches every call.

Recording what happened is table stakes. The judge grades it — scoring every call against your policy the instant it lands, so a breach is caught in the moment, not in a post-mortem.

Every call your app makes is scored in real time against the policy you tagged it with. A separate judge model reads the interaction and returns a verdict — clean, or flagged with a reason — which gets sealed into the chain alongside the record.

What it does for you

  • Real-time grading: policy checks run as calls happen, against HIPAA, SOC 2, or your own rules.
  • Instant flags: anything crossing the line is surfaced the moment it happens.
  • Verdict on the record: the judge's decision is part of the tamper-evident chain, not a separate note.
  • You see it live: the desktop app goes red with the real reason the instant a breach is graded.
