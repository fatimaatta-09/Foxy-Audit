About

Built by people who've been audited.

Our mission is simple: make AI compliance something you can prove, not just promise — a tamper-evident trail for every model call that anyone can verify without trusting us.

Teams are shipping AI faster than they can prove it's behaving. Foxy Audit closes that gap — one decorator turns every AI call into a cryptographically verifiable record, so when a customer, an auditor, or a regulator asks "can you show us?", the answer is yes.
