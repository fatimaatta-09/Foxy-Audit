About

Built by people who've been audited.

Our mission is simple: make AI compliance evidence something you can verify, not just promise — a tamper-evident trail for interactions your team captures through the SDK.

Teams are shipping AI faster than they can prove what their instrumented systems did. Foxy Audit closes that evidence gap — one decorator turns a captured interaction into a cryptographically verifiable record, so when a customer, auditor, or regulator asks "can you show us?", the answer is evidence, not a promise.
